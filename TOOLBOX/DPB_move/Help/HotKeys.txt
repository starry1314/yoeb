DreamPoeBot Default Hotkeys
Last updated: July 29, 2026

These shortcuts must be pressed while the Path of Exile window has focus.
Configurable shortcuts may be changed or disabled in DreamPoeBot settings.

Start or stop the bot: Alt+Shift+S
Force-stop the bot: Ctrl+Shift+S
Focus the DreamPoeBot window: Alt+Shift+W
Log the mouse cursor position: Alt+Shift+X

Enable ProcessHookManager: Alt+Shift+E
Disable ProcessHookManager: Alt+Shift+D
Reset ProcessHookManager key states: Alt+Shift+R

Dump the target under the player: Alt+Shift+T
Dump the UI frame under the cursor: Alt+Shift+A
Dump the Delve cell under the cursor: Alt+Shift+L
Toggle diagnostic rendering: Alt+Shift+Z
